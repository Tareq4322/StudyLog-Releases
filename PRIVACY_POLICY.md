# StudyLog - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 2026

We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy details the types of information StudyLog collects, processes, and stores, and how we protect it.

---

## 1. Information We Collect & Process

### A. Account Information (Cloud Sync)
If you explicitly sign in with Google to enable Cloud Sync:
* We receive your Google profile name, email address, and profile photo URL.
* This data is used solely to authenticate your account and securely synchronize your academic records (courses, grades, notes, tasks) across your devices.

### B. User-Generated Data
The courses, tasks, notes, and grades you enter are stored locally in a private database on your device. If Cloud Sync is enabled, this data is sent securely to our Firebase servers for backup.

### C. System Calendar Data (Calendar Sync)
If you grant the optional Calendar permissions (Read/Write Calendar):
* StudyLog reads and writes your course schedules directly to your device's native calendar.
* **THIS CALENDAR DATA IS PROCESSED STRICTLY ON-DEVICE.** We never upload your calendar events or details to our servers.

### D. Screen Time Usage Data (Screen Time Tracker)
If you grant the optional Usage Access permission:
* StudyLog aggregates app usage durations using Android's `UsageStatsManager` API to help you visualize screen time statistics.
* **THIS DATA IS PROCESSED STRICTLY ON-DEVICE.** Screen usage details, app names, and activity logs are never sent to our servers or shared with third parties.

---

## 2. Data Sharing & Third Parties
We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or companies. Data is only transferred to Google Firebase services for authentication and database synchronization.

---

## 3. Data Retention & Deletion Rights
Under GDPR, CCPA, and Google Play Store policies, you have complete control over your data:
* **Clear Cloud Data:** You can delete all synchronized cloud records at any time in Settings.
* **Delete Account:** You can permanently delete your authenticated account and all related data in Settings.
* **Local Data:** Uninstalling the App immediately purges all local databases from your device.

---

## 4. Security
We implement industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest within Firebase. However, no transmission method is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
